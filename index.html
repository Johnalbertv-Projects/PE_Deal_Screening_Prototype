How it works

  1. 1Deal Document
  2. 2Structured Extraction
  3. 3Investment Criteria
  4. 4Screening Assessment
  5. 5Human Review
  • Synthetic sources. Every deal document is fictional and created for this prototype.
  • Structured data. Facts are extracted from the source document into a normalized record, each with its page reference.
  • Gaps preserved. Missing or unclear information is recorded as missing or ambiguous rather than guessed.
  • Independent criteria. Each investment criterion is evaluated separately as Met, Not met, or Insufficient information.
  • Human review. Results support preliminary screening only; people make the investment decisions.
